About Me

Hi, I’m Anna! I’m a Full-Stack Developer based in Espoo, Finland. I wanted to be a programmer since I was a kid, so I studied Mathematics and Computer Science and got a Master’s degree. Then life took another turn, and for nine years I worked in SEO and digital marketing. That job showed me how real people use websites and apps, and I still think about it when I build things.

Later I came back to coding. In 2026 I did a backend internship at Ellarion Tales, a real client project: a registration system for LARP events, made for 1000+ participants. I designed the PostgreSQL database, built the REST API, and chose Render instead of Azure because my estimate showed it was 2.4x cheaper.

Now I build AI projects with the Claude API: a shopping assistant with tool use and MCP, and a financial coach that my team and I made at the Junction hackathon. I use AI tools to work faster, but I make the decisions about architecture and quality myself.

I moved to Finland in 2022 and learned Finnish to B2 level while changing my career. It showed me that I can adapt fast and work with international teams. I try to write code the next person won't curse me for, because what we write today is someone else's responsibility tomorrow.

I also take part in tech and career communities, like Espoo Talent Hub, Women in Tech and Womento. Outside of work I practice Finnish at a local kielikahvila, do yoga and make music.
