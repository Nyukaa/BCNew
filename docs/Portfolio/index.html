About Me

Hi, I’m Anna! Ever since I was a kid, I dreamed of becoming a programmer, which led me to a Master’s degree in Mathematics and Computer Science. Life had its own twists, though, and I spent my first professional chapter in SEO and digital marketing, learning exactly how real people interact with apps and websites.

Eventually, I returned to coding. Today, I combine my math background with a marketing mindset to deliver real value to both the end-users and the product owners. For example, at Ellarion, I built the backend for a platform with 1000+ users, designed the PostgreSQL database, and cut deployment costs by 2x by choosing Render over Azure.

I moved to Finland four years ago, learned Finnish to a B2 level, and switched my career into tech. It proved to me that I can adapt fast and work well in any team. I try to write code the next person won't curse me for - because what we write today is someone else's responsibility tomorrow.

Right now, I’m focused on my next career step and actively diving into the local tech scene-building pet projects, learning AI technologies, joining meetups, and prepping for an upcoming hackathon. I also spend time practicing my Finnish at a local kielikahvila, doing yoga, and making music. Let's connect and build something cool together!
